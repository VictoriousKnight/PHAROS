What happens during analysis?

  1. The upload is validated and bounded.
  2. File type and document structure are identified.
  3. Security-relevant features are extracted.
  4. Detection and ML analysis are performed.
  5. CDR policy determines whether content should be preserved, sanitized, removed, reviewed, or rejected.
  6. A report and, where applicable, sanitized output are generated.
